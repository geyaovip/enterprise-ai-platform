# Drawing sheets and harness detail tables

- Locate the actual table header and its repeated body; headings may appear below a small table. A title block with designer/date/scale/material does not establish a multi-component BOM.
- Drawing balloons and wiring/pin tables are cross-reference evidence only. They cannot supply missing procurement rows or quantities.
- Keep the quantity cell, length cell and descriptive length notation separate. A row may have an explicit count plus a separate cut length, or a length-based consumption quantity with an explicit length unit. Use the printed column roles, not the size of the number.
- Preserve an as-required quantity literally while leaving the adopted numeric quantity null. If a count cell is blank but the description contains a length, do not move that length into the count cell or its raw quantity field.
- Do not apply a drawing-wide dimensional unit to component counts. Retain the printed per-row quantity unit; an absent per-row unit stays absent.
