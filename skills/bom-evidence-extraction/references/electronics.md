# Electronic parts tables

- Map internal code, manufacturer part number, supplier stock code, manufacturer, reference designators, value, footprint and population status separately. A PCB or mechanical accessory identifier printed in the PartNumber column is still source content: preserve it without deciding whether it is an orderable semiconductor MPN.
- A manufacturer-part cell can contain multiple alternatives. Keep the full original alternatives and manufacturer list; neither choose one nor multiply the row quantity by the number of alternatives.
- Rejoin a wrapped identifier only within the same row and column. Check its prefix and final suffix against the image once. Do not reconstruct a missing character from familiarity with a part family.
- Preserve every reference, including punctuation or a nonstandard prefix. Do not change a leading symbol into a letter, or add/remove references to agree with Quantity. A reference-count discrepancy is a review issue, not a correction instruction.
- Fitted, not fitted, zero quantity, a missing MPN and an explicit not-to-buy note are different facts. Retain zero and unfitted rows as source evidence without making them positive procurement demand. A source-missing identifier stays null; do not recover it from a title, circuit function or catalogue knowledge.
