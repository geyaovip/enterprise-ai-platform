# Mechanical and configuration tables

- Internal material code, part name, revision and material specification are separate source roles. A blank revision is not permission to copy the previous revision.
- Detect multiple configuration quantity columns by their headings. Preserve each heading/value pair, including explicit zero and blank. Without a selected configuration, do not emit a single adopted quantity, even if two columns happen to agree.
- Totals, revision notes and parent assembly titles are not extra component rows. Do not multiply a quantity through an unstated parent relationship.
- Material properties, hardness and colour remain specification facts. They must not become quantities or versions. A part-name-only source can provide a name candidate, not a newly invented enterprise material identifier.
